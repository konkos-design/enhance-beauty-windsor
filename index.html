Shaped for your face

The quiet maths behind a good brow.

A brow that suits you follows your own features, not a trend. These are the classic proportions every shape is checked against:

  1. 1Where it startsStraight up from the side of the nose.
  2. 2Where it liftsOn a line from the nose through the centre of the eye.
  3. 3Where it endsOn a line from the nose past the outer corner of the eye.
