Lash lift & tint

Your natural lashes are lifted from the root and tinted, so they look longer and darker without anything added. It's the service Batoul is best known for, and plenty of her clients have booked it regularly for years.

  • No eyelash curler, no falsies, no extensions
  • Low to no maintenance once it's done
  • Around half an hour in the chair
  • Tint adds colour, so many clients skip mascara

Brow lamination & tint

Lamination sets your brow hairs in a brushed-up shape, so brows look fuller and stay where they should. A tint adds definition and a wax cleans up the edges.

  • Brushed-up, fuller-looking brows
  • Usually paired with a tint and a tidy-up wax
  • Works well for sparse or unruly brows
  • Can be combined with a lash lift in one visit

Microblading

Microblading adds fine, hair-like strokes where brows are thin or missing. Batoul takes the time to plan the shape with you and explain the process, which is why nervous first-timers keep recommending her.

  • Shape and colour planned with you first
  • Hair-like strokes for sparse, gappy or faded brows
  • Colour matched to your skin and hair
  • Every step explained before anything starts
